Downloads

It's all free to reuse: CC BY 4.0 for the data and text, MIT for the code. Just credit Andi Sharkey (2026). The full repository, scripts and all, is at https://github.com/OneRedOdd/scifi-timeline.

Data

  • films.csv (114 KB): 233 films: release year, setting year, gap, format, release type, country, director, tone, source and notes
  • predictions.csv (62 KB): every prediction, scored or excluded, with its verdict, reason and source
  • alt_history_candidates.csv (1 KB): alternate-history films set aside
  • rejected_candidates.csv (3 KB): candidates rejected under the inclusion rules
  • series.csv (19 KB): 94 TV series set aside
  • match_mine_vs_wl.csv (17 KB): my 163-film snapshot matched to Wythoff & Leane (attributed excerpts, CC BY 4.0)
  • add_candidates_shortlist.csv (32 KB): Wythoff & Leane films missing from my 163, before I checked them
  • README.md (9 KB): codebook for every column
