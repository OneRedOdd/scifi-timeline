Predictions: what came true?

For the 81 films whose setting year has already come round, I picked out the main on-screen technology and society forecasts. Each one is scored came true, sort of or didn't, judged by the film's own year. That's 266 rows, and 220 of them are scored. The rest are left out because they're plot devices, or because the thing already existed when the film came out.

Before you quote any of this
  • These are well-known films that name a year, not a random sample of science fiction. So nothing here means "science fiction gets X% right".
  • I did the scoring myself and checked my work twice. There's no second scorer, though, so there's no inter-rater reliability statistic.
  • Gap vs accuracy: films set further ahead did score slightly better, but that largely fades once you account for the theme of the prediction (audit v1, 164-film version; I haven't rerun it on 233 yet). So it's not "no link", but it's not evidence that looking further ahead makes a film more accurate either.
  • "Dystopias are rising" only holds on Wikipedia's fairly generous labels (p = 0.012). On the stricter on-screen rule the trend isn't significant (p = 0.24).
  • Most theme and tone groups have fewer than 30 predictions, so I wouldn't read much into the small ones.
