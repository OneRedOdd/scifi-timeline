Comparison with Wythoff & Leane (2025)

Wythoff and Leane's Time Horizons of Futuristic Fiction hand-checked 2,552 works set in the future, 960 of them films. It's the closest thing to my project I've found, so I checked my list against it. This was when my list stood at 163 films (11 October 2026), before it grew to 233.

Mine turns out to be mostly a subset of theirs: 146 of my 163 films (90%) are in their data. The 17 they don't have are mostly 2023–26 releases (their data stops in early 2025), plus a few obscure early titles.

W&L and I agree on the setting year for 132 of the 146 shared films. Of the 14 disagreements, 6 come from their method (they take the median of a multi-year story where I use the main year), 5 are source conflicts I'd already flagged, and 3 look like errors in their data when checked against current Wikipedia.

On look-ahead, their 960 films (959 with a year) have a median gap of 25 years against my 32. But on the shared films both datings give about 31, so the difference is down to which films each list picked. Theirs includes lots of superhero, horror and comedy films set a year or two ahead.

Their films do show a trend. The median look-ahead falls from about 50 years in the 1960s to 17 in the 2010s and about 10 in the early 2020s. Novels don't show this, and my own list shows no clear trend. Part of the effect is the incidental near future of franchise films, rather than film-makers imagining less far ahead.

Their data also turned up films I'd missed. 51 of them were checked and added when the list grew to 233.

The full write-up is in findings.md. The matched table is match_mine_vs_wl.csv, and the shortlist is add_candidates_shortlist.csv.

Their raw data isn't redistributed here. You can get futuristic_fiction.csv from the Post45 Data Collective repository (CC BY 4.0).
