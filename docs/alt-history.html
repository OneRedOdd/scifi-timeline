Alternate history (set aside)

Films whose world breaks away from real history before they came out fail my "real timeline" rule. So they're not in the 233. I've parked them to look at later.

So far only one film has been moved out: 2009: Lost Memories (2002), where Korea is still under Imperial Japanese rule in 2009 after a 1909 assassination goes differently. It came out when I first checked my work. Its two prediction rows are still in predictions.csv, marked unscored. The plan for the follow-on is a chart of the real year each story breaks from history.
